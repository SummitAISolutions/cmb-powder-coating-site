## Notes

- **Sourcing status.** No real CMB photography or logo file is authorised yet (facts.md open questions: Instagram/media-library photos, logo files). Every placed image is generated supporting imagery of materials, light and place — never a part, the shop, a person or a vehicle.
- **Generation.** Alibaba Model Studio `qwen-image-2.0-pro` and its dated snapshots (rotated on rate limits), synchronous calls, `prompt_extend: false`, `watermark: false`, one attempt per slot. Candidates were converted to WebP (quality 80) at their generated size; Astro builds the responsive widths. Provenance (model snapshot, request id, prompt, date, size, image hash) is handed to the imagery stage for `visual/asset-manifest.json`.
- **Reuse.** Six images serve every page: each slot lists the pages and sections that use it. Openings and closing panels reuse the series rather than adding near-duplicates.
- **Recent work.** The direction's "Recent work" band is omitted, not filled, until the owner authorises real photographs (`recent-work`, needs-asset). The service cards switch to real photos at the same point.
- **Proof strip.** The rating/review strip under the home opening is not an image slot; it stays unrendered until the owner authorises review display (INPUT_MANIFEST.md).
- **Alt text.** All generated images are decorative: `alt=""`, never captioned, never presented as CMB's work or premises.
- **Logo.** The header and footer set the business name in Barlow Condensed from the entity until an approved logo file exists.
