# Reference Analysis

> Curate **3–6** references. Each is analysed **by axis**, so we extract
> principles rather than a page to clone. A reference may contribute to only one
> axis — say so, and leave the others as "not taken".
>
> A reference is never permission to copy. It never supplies client facts,
> copy, imagery or proof.

## Reference 1 — Jace Cox and Modern Necessity (Summit builds, r4/r5 and r2)

### Source

`SummitAISolutions/jace-cox-site` (r4/r5) and `SummitAISolutions/modern-necessity-site`
(r2, `visual/visual-direction.md`), both approved by Samuel Wade on 2026-10-05.
Curated by the visual director, 2026-10-05.

### Typography

Very large display headings set straight onto imagery, one lede, small uppercase
eyebrows in the body face. Taken as a principle; the faces are not.

### Layout / Composition

Floating rounded header card over full-bleed openings; words on a scrim, not in
a box; frosted panels; tall image cards; a closing panel with its own image on
every page; a dark footer with the Summit credit; a sticky mobile call bar.

### Color

One saturated colour reserved for the call action; everything else neutral.
Taken as a rule; the colours are not.

### Imagery

One consistently graded generated series of places, light and materials; never
people, work or premises; empty alt; never captioned as the client's.

### Interaction / Motion

Mobile menu opens as a floating panel over the page. Minimal motion.

### KEEP

- Imagery behind words, not framed images laid over heroes.
- No divider lines between topics; no numerals unless there is a real order.
- Proof directly under the opening; the CTA is the emphasis.
- Openers and closers share one grammar.

### DIFFER

- Neither practice's teal/iodine or navy, its serif, nor its subjects. CMB is a
  trade shop: condensed industrial type, primer-white and graphite surfaces,
  amber action, metal and light instead of rooms and landscapes.
- A quote request is a first-class second action (Modern Necessity and Jace are
  call-led only).

### Fit

Samuel approved this grammar twice on 2026-10-05 and rejected the
typography-only alternative ("I genuinely don't really like it that much").
CMB has no authorised photography yet, so the same "imagery behind words"
approach lets the site look finished without fabricating proof.

## Reference 2 — Grill Docs design-taste build (Summit, trade service)

### Source

`SummitAISolutions/grilldocs-design-taste` (BUILD_REPORT.md, facts.md §19–24,
`src/styles.css`). Curated by the visual director, 2026-10-05.

### Typography

Condensed, workshop-weight headings for a hands-on trade. Taken as a direction;
Archivo itself is not reused (another client's identity).

### Layout / Composition

Every customer page opens with its own relevant image; every section has a
focal point (cards, checklists, figure bands) — no plain text strips.

### Color

Samuel's correction "too much black": surfaces rotate so no two adjacent
sections share one. Taken.

### Imagery

Image precedence: authentic photography > brand assets > generated supporting
images. Taken.

### Interaction / Motion

Not taken.

### KEEP

- Rotate primer / graphite / steel-tint surfaces; never two adjacent alike.
- Every section has a focal point; no plain text strips.

### DIFFER

- Not dark-dominant: light primer is the base surface, graphite is a band.
- No flame orange; CMB's accent comes from its own current site and logo.

### Fit

Closest prior build in trade and audience (hands-on, Southern Idaho, a shop
that works on things people own).
